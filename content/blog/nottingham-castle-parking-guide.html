10 Nottingham Castle Parking Options and Tips

Nottingham Castle has no on-site parking for visitors. That is the single most important fact to know before you leave home. The historic gates open directly into pedestrianised grounds, and the surrounding streets are monitored closely by the city council. Arriving without a plan means circling the one-way system and paying whatever the first meter demands.

This guide covers every realistic option within a 10-minute walk, from the large Broad Marsh facility on Canal Street to quiet residential bays in NG1 and NG2. Pricing, height restrictions, EV charging, and Blue Badge details are drawn from official sources, including the Nottingham Castle getting here page and the Transport Nottingham Broad Marsh guide. Check our Nottingham Castle visiting guide for admission prices and opening times before you set off.

Nottingham Castle On-Site Parking Policy

Nottingham Castle does not permit on-site parking for the general public. The historic grounds are strictly pedestrianised to protect the archaeology beneath, and vehicular access is restricted to pre-arranged deliveries and service vehicles only. This applies year-round, including on quieter weekdays when the castle is less crowded.

Dropping off passengers directly at the main gate on Castle Road is difficult because the road is narrow and under camera surveillance. However, there is a dropped kerb with tactile paving at the top of Castle Road that works for brief drop-offs. Coaches use Peveril Drive (NG7 1DE) for scheduled set-downs.

The castle sits on a hill, and the steepest approach path carries a 1 in 10 gradient. If you are pushing a stroller, using a wheelchair, or have limited mobility, factor this into which car park you choose. Broad Marsh, with its lifts and flat exit routes toward Middle Hill, is the most accessible option for most visitors.

Before you commit to a full day of parking, review the Nottingham Castle tickets and prices to calculate your total spend. The walk from most nearby car parks takes between five and fifteen minutes depending on which exit you use.

Broad Marsh Car Park (Canal Street)

Broad Marsh is the main car park for castle visitors and the best all-around choice. The entrance is on Canal Street, which you reach via London Road. There are 1,304 spaces total across six floors, including 1,200 standard bays, 38 Blue Badge bays on floors 2 and 3, and 90 motorcycle spaces. The height restriction is 2.2 metres (7 feet 3 inches).

Pricing ranges from around £4.50 for two hours up to £16.00 for a full day, though these rates are subject to change — check the digital displays at the entrance for current figures. There is a 20-minute grace period for passenger drop-offs and pick-ups, which is useful if you are meeting someone at the adjacent bus station. Five lifts serve the lower ground floor, with exits to both Middle Hill and Carrington Street.

For EV drivers, Broad Marsh has 81 fast charge points operated by bp pulse via the D2N2 charging network, all powered by renewable energy. A mix of 7 kW and 22 kW chargers are spread across floors 2 to 5. You pay through the bp pulse app or website. This is the largest single-site EV installation in the UK, making it the obvious choice if you are arriving in an electric vehicle.

From a sustainability standpoint, 720 solar panels on the roof are expected to save more than 53 tonnes of carbon per year. The ventilation system is sensor-controlled, and the building holds an Energy Performance Certificate 'A' rating. If these details matter to you as an eco-conscious driver, Broad Marsh is the only car park in central Nottingham with this level of green credentials.

Blue Badge Parking on Castle Road

There are 3 designated Blue Badge spaces on Castle Road directly outside the castle entrance (NG1 6AA), plus 12 regular spaces nearby and additional street parking near the college. All Castle Road Blue Badge spaces are free of charge for badge holders. These are the closest spaces to the gate, but they fill quickly — aim to arrive before 09:30 to secure one.

The path from Castle Road to the main entrance does involve some incline, but the castle team can assist you to the top if needed. There is lift access inside Robin Hood Adventures, and the museum building itself is fully wheelchair accessible with ramped areas marked throughout. A Changing Places toilet is located in the Gatehouse next to the Visitor Centre, and two accessible toilets are in the Visitor Centre itself.

If the Castle Road spaces are full, St James' Street Car Park (NG1 6EY) is the recommended overflow for Blue Badge holders, though it is charged at standard rates. The Broad Marsh facility also has 38 dedicated Blue Badge bays on floors 2 and 3, which is a more reliable bet on busy days. Check the full accessibility guide at the official Nottingham Castle accessibility page before your visit.

St James' Street Car Park

St James' Street Car Park (NG1 6EY) is the primary alternative for Blue Badge holders when Castle Road spaces are full. It is a multi-story facility in the heart of the city center, well-suited for visitors who want to combine the castle with a trip to the Old Market Square or nearby cultural venues. The walk to the castle is mostly flat from this direction.

Blue Badge holders can park here at standard rates — unlike Castle Road, this is not a free site. Daily rates average around £15.00, with hourly options available for shorter stays. The central location makes it useful if you are arriving from the north side of the city or coming in via Maid Marian Way.

Q-Park Nottingham Central

Q-Park Nottingham Central on Talbot Street (also listed as Q-Park Talbot Street on booking platforms) is a premium multi-story option with spacious bays suited to larger family SUVs. Security is high, the bays are generously sized, and the facility is consistently well-maintained. It is a reliable choice when Broad Marsh is showing a full sign.

Standard drive-in rates can reach £20.00 for a full day, making this one of the pricier options. Pre-booking online through the Q-Park website typically saves 15–20% and guarantees a space, which is worth doing during school holidays or when a major event is scheduled nearby. The walk to the castle from Talbot Street takes around 8–10 minutes.

Premier Inn Nottingham Castle Marina

The Castle Marina area, served by the Premier Inn lot off Castle Bridge Road (NG7 1GX), is a strong alternative for visitors arriving from the south or west via the A453 or Clifton Boulevard. You bypass the congested city center approach entirely, which cuts journey time considerably during peak hours. The canal-side walk into the city takes around 15 minutes and passes some pleasant waterside scenery.

Parking here is generally cheaper than the city center multi-stories, and the area is quieter. Note that sections of the lot are reserved for hotel guests and permit holders — check the signage carefully and use only the publicly designated bays. JustPark lists pre-bookable spaces in this zone from around £6.00 per day for nearby private driveways.

Cumberland Place Car Park

Cumberland Place Car Park off Mount Street is a quieter alternative when Broad Marsh is full. The walk to the castle is mostly flat and takes around 8 minutes, passing through the lower edge of The Park residential area. It is a good option if you are arriving from Maid Marian Way or the Derby Road direction.

Standard hourly rates apply, usually around £3.50 per hour, with all-day options at competitive rates. There are no notable height restrictions that would exclude standard passenger vehicles. This car park rarely appears on basic searches but is listed on JustPark and Google Maps for those who look.

Sainsbury's Castle Boulevard

Sainsbury's on Castle Boulevard offers short-stay parking that can work for quick stops near the castle. The supermarket operates a grace period for customers who make a qualifying purchase in-store, which can provide two hours at no charge. ANPR cameras monitor the lot strictly, and exceeding the grace period or stay limit results in an automatic fine — there is no flexibility.

This option is best suited for visitors doing a short drop-off run or picking up supplies before heading to the castle grounds. It is not practical for a full-day visit. Always verify the current terms on the signage at the entrance, as the grace period and minimum spend threshold can change.

Parking on Arkwright Walk (NG2)

The Arkwright Walk area in NG2, just south of the canal, offers private driveways and residential bays that can be booked through apps like JustPark. Prices are often significantly lower than commercial lots — sometimes as low as £5.00–£6.00 for a full day. The walk to the castle entrance takes around 10 minutes via the canal towpath.

Pre-booking is essential here, as availability changes daily. Search for spaces on JustPark filtering by NG2 to see current listings. This is one of the best-value options in 2026 for visitors who plan ahead, particularly for full-day or multi-hour visits.

Parking on College Street (NG1)

College Street on the north side of the castle offers on-street bays that tend to be quieter on weekends when the adjacent college and offices are closed. The spaces sit close to the castle's north approach and are a good choice if you are coming in from the A60 or the cathedral area. Walk time to the main gate is under 10 minutes.

Pay-and-display meters apply here on weekday business hours, and some bays carry local resident permit restrictions. Check the meter face carefully before paying. These bays fill up more slowly than Castle Road, making them a reliable fallback when the obvious options are full.

Quick Cost Comparison

Here is a snapshot of approximate 2026 rates for the most-used options near the castle. Prices fluctuate and should be verified at the entrance or booking platform before your visit.

  • Broad Marsh (Canal Street): £4.50 for 2 hours, £16.00 full day — 81 EV chargers, 38 Blue Badge bays, height limit 2.2m
  • Castle Road on-street bays: free for Blue Badge (3 spaces), approx £4.00 for 2 hours for standard — 8am to 8pm daily
  • St James' Street Car Park: approx £15.00 full day — closest alternative for Blue Badge overflow
  • Q-Park Nottingham Central: up to £20.00 full day — pre-book online for 15–20% saving
  • Cumberland Place off Mount Street: approx £3.50 per hour — quieter, flat walk to castle
  • Arkwright Walk private driveways (NG2): from £5.00–£6.00 all day via JustPark — book in advance

For reference, the on-street bays closest to the gate have the shortest walk but the earliest fill time. Broad Marsh is the most reliable for full-day visits, especially for families and EV drivers. If you want to add more Nottingham activities to your day, Broad Marsh is the best base since it connects directly to the bus station.

What to Avoid

The Victoria Centre car park is the largest in Nottingham but is located on the opposite side of the city center from the castle. Walking from there adds 20 minutes each way through crowded shopping streets. It is convenient for the market area, not for the castle.

Avoid parking near the Motorpoint Arena when a major event is on. Event-rate pricing can double the standard hourly charge at surrounding lots with little warning, and the exit routes become bottlenecks. Check the arena events calendar when planning your visit.

Small private lots near the canal can appear on navigation apps but may have misleading signage about which machine serves which bays. Stick to council-run car parks and well-known operators like Q-Park or pre-booked JustPark spaces. Unexpected fines are the most common complaint from first-time visitors to this part of the city.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are the Blue Badge spaces for Nottingham Castle?

There are 3 designated Blue Badge spaces on Castle Road directly near the entrance. If these are full, 12 additional regular spaces are nearby, or you can use the St James' Street Car Park. Always check the latest nottingham castle parking accessibility guide for updates.

How much does parking cost near Nottingham Castle?

Typical costs range from £4.00 for a two-hour on-street stay to £16.00 for a full day at Broad Marsh. Multi-story car parks like Q-Park may charge up to £20.00 for premium security and larger spaces. Check our Nottingham sightseeing guide for more budget tips.

Is there EV charging near the castle?

Yes, the Broad Marsh Car Park on Canal Street offers 81 electric vehicle charging points. This is the most reliable location for EV owners visiting the city center. The facility is open 24/7 and is just a short walk from the castle gates.

Finding the right Nottingham Castle parking is straightforward once you know the options. Broad Marsh on Canal Street handles the vast majority of visitor needs — it has the most spaces, the best EV charging, dedicated Blue Badge bays, and a flat exit route that avoids the steepest part of Castle Road. If it is full, Cumberland Place and St James' Street are solid backups within the same walking distance.

Plan your parking the same way you plan your entry ticket: book in advance for Q-Park, pre-reserve a JustPark driveway in NG2 for the best value, and allow extra time if you are relying on the on-street bays closest to the gate. The castle and its caves are worth every minute of the walk.
