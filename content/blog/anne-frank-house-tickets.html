Anne Frank House Tickets: 7 Things to Know Before Booking

Is the Anne Frank House Worth Visiting?

Quick Answer: The best way to get tickets is via the official site every Tuesday at 10:00 AM. Regular adult tickets cost €16.50 / ~$18 and must be booked about six weeks in advance.

The museum is one of the most significant Amsterdam Landmarks: 12 Essential Sites to Visit for history lovers. Walking through the movable bookcase into the Secret Annex is a haunting experience. It brings the pages of the Diary of a Young Girl to life vividly. Most visitors spend about an hour reflecting on the tragic events of World War II.

You should prepare for a somber atmosphere and very narrow, steep staircases. The house remains largely empty of furniture, as requested by Otto Frank. This emptiness emphasizes the loss felt by the families who hid there. Seeing the original diary in the museum is a powerful moment for many.

If you appreciate historical context, this is arguably the best museum in Amsterdam to visit. The museum provides a free audio guide to explain every room in detail. It helps you understand the daily lives of those in hiding under Nazi occupation. The experience is educational and emotionally taxing but ultimately very rewarding.

Wait times for entry are usually short if you have a pre-booked time slot. However, the rooms are small and can feel quite crowded during peak hours. Visiting in the evening often provides a slightly quieter environment for reflection. The museum remains open until 10:00 PM most nights to accommodate more guests.

How to Get Anne Frank House Tickets (Step-by-Step)

Planning your visit requires precision because tickets are only available online. You cannot buy them at the door under any circumstances anymore. The official website is the only legitimate source for purchasing your entry. Secondary markets often sell invalid tickets at highly inflated prices.

Before you start, decide if you want the introductory program for extra context. This 30-minute talk costs an additional €7 / ~$8 per person. It is excellent for those who want a deeper dive into the history. The program takes place just before your scheduled entry into the house.

Compare your options: A regular ticket costs €16.50 / ~$18 for adults. Introductory tickets cost €23.50 / ~$25 and include the 30-minute historical presentation. Both options grant full access to the Secret Annex and museum exhibits. Children under nine pay only €1 / ~$1.10 for their museum entry.

    • Visit the official website exactly six weeks before your planned arrival date.
    • Entry costs €16.50 / ~$18 and the museum stays open until 10:00 PM.
    • Checking too early will show no availability for your future travel dates.
  2. Step 2: Set an alarm for Tuesday morning
    • New ticket batches always release on Tuesdays at 10:00 AM Amsterdam time.
    • Expect to spend 15 minutes in a digital queue during peak seasons.
    • Failing to log in right at 10:00 AM often results in sold-out dates.
  3. Step 3: Choose your specific ticket type
    • Select either the regular entry or the ticket with the introductory program.
    • The introductory program adds €7 / ~$8 to your total booking cost.
    • Booking the wrong type might mean missing the historical talk you wanted.
  4. Step 4: Select an available time slot
    • Pick a time that fits your itinerary, preferably early or very late.
    • Most slots are for 15-minute intervals throughout the operating day.

If you cannot enter the house, consider the Anne Frank’s Last Walk VR Tour. This virtual experience allows you to see the Secret Annex in detail. It is a great option for those with limited mobility who cannot climb stairs. The VR tour provides a high-quality alternative that is very respectful.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy Anne Frank House tickets at the door?

No, you cannot buy tickets at the door. All tickets must be purchased online via the official website. This policy ensures the museum remains safe and uncrowded for all visitors.

How long does a visit to the Anne Frank House take?

A typical visit takes about 60 to 90 minutes. This includes time for the audio tour and the museum exhibits. If you add the introductory program, plan for two hours total.

What happens if I miss my entry time slot?

The museum is very strict about entry times. If you arrive late, you may be denied entry without a refund. Always aim to arrive at the Prinsengracht entrance 10 minutes early.
